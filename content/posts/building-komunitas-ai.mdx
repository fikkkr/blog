KOMUNITAS is an AI-related project I worked on as part of LKS Nasional 2026.

The project gave me a reason to learn unfamiliar tools and think more carefully about what a useful feature should do. The backend context includes Bun, Hono, TypeScript, and Supabase.

## Learning Through the Project

Working on a project for a competition changes the way I learn. I cannot stop at knowing the name of a technology. I need to understand enough of the code to make decisions, find mistakes, and explain what I built.

There were parts I understood quickly and parts that needed more reading and experiments. That is normal for where I am right now.

## AI Is a Tool, Not a Shortcut Around Understanding

I use AI to explore possibilities, generate a starting point, and help me look at a problem from another angle. After that, I still need to check the result and understand how it fits into the project.

The useful lesson from KOMUNITAS is not that I already know everything about AI. It is that building something real gives me better questions to ask.

## What I Am Taking Forward

I want to keep practising the same cycle: try something, run into a problem, investigate it, understand it, and rebuild with more clarity.
