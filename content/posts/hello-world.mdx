I wanted a place to collect the things I am learning instead of keeping every project in a separate corner.

I am still a student. This website is not meant to make me look like a senior engineer. It is a digital home for projects, notes, experiments, and the bugs that taught me something.

## Build, Break, Understand, Rebuild

The part I enjoy most is usually not the first successful result. It is the moment something stops working and I have to investigate why.

I learn better by trying a feature, getting stuck, reading documentation, changing something, and checking what actually happened. Memorizing a solution is useful for a moment; understanding the reason behind it lasts longer.

## Using AI Without Handing Over Understanding

AI can help me explore an idea, generate boilerplate, or get unstuck. It does not remove the need to read the code and understand what it is doing.

That is the standard I want to keep for this site too: build honestly, document what I know, and leave uncertain details out.

## What Comes Next

I will keep using this space for notes about web development, debugging, school projects, and experiments with new tools.
